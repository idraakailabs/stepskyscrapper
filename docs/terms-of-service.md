## 2. What the app does

The app turns the steps you walk into a city: every 1,000 steps adds a floor to a skyscraper, and finished towers form
your skyline. Steps come from Health Connect or, where that isn't available, your phone's step counter. There is no
account; your progress is stored on your device.

- Stay aware of your surroundings while walking. Don't use the app in a way that puts you or others at risk.

  problems. We won't take back items you have already unlocked with Blueprints. Premium items that come with the Architect
