Motion principles

Short and physical, like a model piece set down. Reduce-motion swaps every move for a 150ms crossfade.
Floor drop420ms · spring(0.6, 400)Falls 24dp, overshoots 1.5dp, settles. Light haptic on contact. Stagger 140ms when several land.
Crown lift760ms · ease-out + settleCrane hook lowers the crown 44dp onto the top slab, one gentle bump, then the cable retracts.
Windows on900ms · random 0–1.4sAt dusk, windows flicker on one by one with a single stutter, like fluorescent tubes.
Number roll250ms · per digitTabular digits roll vertically on sync so the width never jumps.
Sky shift20 min · linearGradient interpolates with local sun time; never animates while the user is looking unless a sync lands.

Onboarding

Target: the user watches today's real steps turn into floors within 30 seconds of install. No account, no paywall, no sign-up wall.

1aPromise

Every 1,000 steps builds a floor

Walk the way you already do. Finished towers join your skyline, and nothing you build is ever taken away.

REVIEW · DESIGN DIRECTOR PASS

What I found & fixed

FIXED · CONSISTENCY Amber was creeping into navigation The first nav pass used an amber active pill. Moved it to a neutral container so amber only ever means progress or the primary action. The crane keeps amber on purpose: it is the thing building your floor.
FIXED · HIERARCHY Skyline and Me didn't answer "how close is the next floor?" Added a persistent next-floor pill (number + 10-segment bar) to the Skyline and Me headers. Now every tab, both widgets and the onboarding payoff show it within one glance.
FIXED · DATA Tower numbering didn't add up Build said "Tower 6" while the skyline holds 5 towers including Marlow Spire. Corrected to Tower 5 and checked totals: 18+20+22+16+24 = 100 floors, used everywhere.
FIXED · LAYOUT Long tower names wrapped into the subtitle Names now sit on one line. Rule for Compose: max 18 characters, autosize 34 → 26sp before ellipsis. Same fix applied to stat rows and chips that wrapped.
CHECKED · 60-SECOND AHA Install → watching your own floors drop: ~25 seconds Two taps (Start building → Allow) and one system dialog, then 1c builds today's real steps. Risk covered: permission declined shows a demo tower plus a "Connect steps" card; Health Connect missing on older Android deep-links to its Play listing before 1b.
CHECKED · TONE No guilt anywhere Copy audit for "streak", "missed", "lost", "behind", "only": none remain. Zero-step and sync-failure states use neutral grey and reassurance ("Your tower is safe"). No red token exists in the theme.
CHECKED · ACCESSIBILITY AA contrast, 48dp targets, no colour-only states Text pairs are 5.4:1 or better. Selection uses check icons and rings. At font scale over 150%, the Build hero shrinks to 200dp and the card area scrolls; the next-floor number never truncates. Tower content description: "Marlow Spire, 14 of 24 floors, next floor in 340 steps."
OPEN · FOR NEXT ROUND Worth testing Whether 24-floor towers (about 3–4 days each at 7,000 steps) feel right, or if the first tower should be 12 floors for a faster first topping-out. Commissioned illustration should replace the procedural towers before launch.
